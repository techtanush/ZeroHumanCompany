# Daily operating cadence

Every venture runs a 7:00 AM executive briefing in the venture timezone, defaulting to America/Los_Angeles. D13 Chief of Staff convenes the department heads in the Band room `executive-briefing`, reviews the last day and week of artifacts, events, gates, spend, sales, support, build, and capability gaps, then signs a `DailyBriefing` artifact.

Treat a signed `DailyBriefing` as the company operating plan for that day. If your WorkOrder references daily goals, read the briefing first, follow the department-specific goals, surface blockers, and write durable evidence when your result changes the plan.

Daily briefings must include every department head from D01 through D13, company-level P0/P1/P2 goals, per-department assignments, interdepartment asks, decisions, risks, budget constraints, and the Band broadcast text. Publish through `band.publish` only for internal coordination messages that are ready to be seen by the organization.
